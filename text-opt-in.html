How you opt in to texts from Kobe Kahne

Kobe Kahne (sole proprietor, Enumclaw, Washington). Business phone line: (253) 308-3934.

The only way to opt in is by phone, on a call you make yourself. Nobody receives a text unless they call this number and press 1 to ask for one.

Step by step

  1. You call (253) 308-3934 and the call is not answered.
  2. An automated voice reads the consent script below.
  3. If you press 1, you have asked for a text. We record your phone number, the time, and that you pressed 1, and then send you one text.
  4. If you press anything else or nothing, no text is sent. You can leave a voicemail instead.

The full consent script (what the caller hears)

"Hi, you've reached Kobe Kahne. Sorry we missed your call. Press 1 if you'd like a text message from Kobe Kahne at this number about your call. It's one text, plus replies if you write back. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Or stay on the line to leave a message."

After pressing 1, the caller hears: "Thanks. A text is on its way. You can also leave a message after the tone."

The text you receive after pressing 1

Kobe Kahne here. Sorry I missed your call. Want a call back, or can I help by text? Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.

Purpose, frequency, cost

Messages are only about the call you made: a reply to your missed call and the conversation that follows if you text back. No marketing or promotional messages are sent. Frequency: one text per call where you press 1, plus replies to messages you send. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help, or call (253) 308-3934.
