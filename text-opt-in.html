How it works

You opt in by calling (253) 308-3934 yourself. If the call goes unanswered, you get one text message about that missed call so you can continue the conversation by text. If you text back, your reply is passed to Kobe so he can call you back.

We never buy, import, or cold-text phone numbers. Only people who called this number receive a text.

Notice callers hear

"Sorry we missed your call at Kobe (demo line). We just sent you a text. You can also leave a message after the tone."

The text you receive

Hey, it's Kobe. Sorry I missed you, I'm tied up. This is the missed-call text I set up for businesses. Want a call back? Reply STOP to opt out.

Frequency, cost, opting out

Usually one message per missed call; replies only if you text back. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop all messages, HELP for help.
