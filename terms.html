Messaging Terms

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Program

Kobe Kahne Missed-Call Text. When you call (253) 308-3934 and the call goes unanswered, you receive one text reply so you can continue the conversation by text. Replies you send are forwarded to the business owner. No marketing messages are sent.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies; usually one message per missed call. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to stop all messages; you will receive one confirmation and nothing further. Reply START to resume. Reply HELP for help, or call (253) 308-3934.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
